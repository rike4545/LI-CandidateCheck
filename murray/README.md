# Murray review: chrismurrayforstatesenate.com

This is an independent, source-by-source review of the factual claims on **Chris Murray for State Senate** (<https://chrismurrayforstatesenate.com/>). It covers the Home, About and Priorities pages as they stood on **November 26, 2026**.

E. Christopher "Chris" Murray (D, WF) beat incumbent Anthony Palumbo (R, C) in New York State Senate District 1 on November 3, 2026, and takes office in January 2027. The site still reads as it did during the campaign.

> Scope: this review tests whether each claim is **verifiable** and **accurate as stated**. It does not rate the candidate, his opponent, or his policy positions. Pure promises ("I will fight for…") are not rated. Factual premises inside a promise are.

## Bottom line

The site's **core claims hold up**. Nothing tested was found to be false. His legal career, his firm, the Lloyd Aquifer case, and the election result are all documented. The problems are **outdated titles and one inconsistent number**:

1. **Past roles read as current.** The site lists him as "Chair" of the Suffolk County Community College trustees and of Citizens Campaign for the Environment, and as president of the Nassau Council of Chambers of Commerce, with no dates. SCCC now lists him as a trustee, with Shirley Coverdale as chair. News coverage calls him a *former* CCE chair. The chambers presidency is documented for 2010.
2. **The tax-swap math is unclear.** The site says a 1% income tax increase on the **top 8%** of earners would pay for the sales-tax cut. In interviews, he described the same group as people earning **over $600,000**. Only about 1.7% of full-year resident filers earn $500,000 or more, so the two descriptions cover very different groups and would raise very different amounts. The **$5.4 billion** cost of the sales-tax cut itself is reasonable: about a quarter of state sales-tax collections.
3. **Cap-and-invest is described as underway.** The site promises to keep "implementing" a program that was drafted but never adopted. The 2026 budget pushed the regulatory deadline to 2028.
4. **Three bio items have no public trail:** founding the Autism Legal Foundation, NYCLU volunteer work, and the presidency of the Mental Health Association of Nassau County. That does not mean they are false. It means they rest on the campaign's word.
5. **The Lloyd Aquifer claim is accurate, with one nuance.** The 2007 DEC denial is real and Murray was counsel of record. The opponents of the well were a coalition of about a dozen groups, not one "environmental group."

## Page-level issues

- **Chronology is mixed.** Post-election headlines ("flipped" the district) sit next to a "Four weeks to go" banner and a request to give "before November 3."
- **Date typos.** Several news and event items are dated "2926" instead of 2026.
- **Titles have no dates.** See finding 1. The Home page also calls him a CCE "board leader" while the About page says "Chair."
- **No sources.** The site cites no filings, decisions or reports. Its only document reference is the NY Health Act bill numbers.

## Recommended follow-ups (to close the ⚪ items)

- Search the NYS Charities Bureau and IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search for an "Autism Legal Foundation" registration, or ask the campaign for its legal name and EIN.
- Ask the Mental Health Association of Nassau County and NYCLU's Nassau chapter to confirm the roles and years.
- Ask the campaign for the revenue estimate behind the 8% / $600,000 figure, and which threshold it uses.
- Archive the site (Wayback Machine) before it changes into an officeholder site.
