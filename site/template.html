LI-CandidateCheck

Source-by-source fact checks of the claims Long Island candidates make on their own campaign websites. Every review uses the same method and rating scale.

These reviews test whether each claim is verifiable and accurate as stated. They do not rate candidates, endorse anyone, or judge policy positions. The two pages are different kinds of documents (an incumbent's record vs. a challenger's biography and platform), so the counts are not a like-for-like score.
✅ Verified
— matches a primary or reliable secondary source.
🟡 Verified with caveat
— true or plausible, but missing context would change a reader's takeaway.
🟠 Overstated / partly accurate
— part is supported; part is inflated, conflated or contradicted.
⚪ Unverified
— no public record found either way. Not a finding that the claim is false.
🔵 Activity claim
— campaign or ongoing activity that public records can't confirm or refute.
❌ False
— contradicted by evidence.
Data and full method: github.com/rike4545/LI-CandidateCheck. Corrections welcome — open an issue with a source. Text and data licensed CC BY 4.0.
